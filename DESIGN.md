# THE ZIDAN EDIT — Direction 02

A personal editorial sales portfolio: the person, the practice, the next conversation.

## Art direction
Ivory #F2EEE6; ink #242321; secondary #6E655D; brick #A43F2B. Newsreader regular/italic for authored editorial presence, Inter for captions, navigation and UI. Four self-hosted Latin WOFF2 assets and their OFL licenses. Asymmetrical cover typography, an oversized real portrait, printed-page rules, margin notes, deliberately varied spreads and a brick-red evidence ledger.

## Motion
Native scrolling. Portrait displacement on desktop; mask reveals; slow editorial seal; magnetic CTA; contextual MEET/PLAY/EMAIL cursor labels. Sticky notes accompany the method index. Companies, services and tools change their editorial content with short transitions. Reduced-motion media preference and a footer control remove motion while preserving every interaction. There is no WebGL dependency in this direction: the portrait is the hero, and the communication path uses a lightweight SVG ribbon.

## Signature moment
An identity card becomes an integrated video screen in the page. The real introduction stays on the site, with native playback, seeking, volume and fullscreen plus an explicit fullscreen control. Escape and Close return to the identity card. The hero also opens the same experience. Video is mounted only after a deliberate play action. The original CV opens in a separate tab.

## Content and assets
Same verified content and actual assets as SIGNAL. No fabricated dates, metrics, employers, awards or testimonials. The creative brief governs claims and contact details; the uploaded CV is retained byte-for-byte despite its previously noted differences. No stock/generated face or generic circular avatar.

## Mobile
Vertical cover, portrait following the headline, compact captions, service index above the article, two-column company index, stacked ledger and video identity. No desktop overlap on small screens. Native modal index supports keyboard and touch.
